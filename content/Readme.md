#### What is this?

A small notes project to share my notes with friends in a simple, interactive way.  
The notes are in Spanish because we are still learning English.

#### Why I made it

Some goals I have:

- Learn new languages
    - Another Germanic rooted like German.
    - And another Latin rooted like Portuguese, Italian, French or Romanian.
- Live abroad
- Get a solid job and better resources for personal projects, family, and hobbies

And honestly, doing it with friends who want the same makes it way more real. So I want to prepare properly and take steps to make it happen.

#### Why Germany?

Short version:

- I like the language and culture
- I like the landscapes
- The tech scene and opportunities look good (including options for Chileans)

But the main reason: it feels like a realistic path to grow professionally, support my parents in Chile, and move closer to what I actually want to do long-term: **Robotics, Automation, or Prosthetics**.
